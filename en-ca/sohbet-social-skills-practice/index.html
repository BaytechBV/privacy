Sohbet: Social Skills Practice

Small talk & confidence coach. Two conversations without a subscription — live conversation, voice mode and full assessment. No account, no email. Now in 41 languages; Pro starts with a one-week trial.

About the app

Sohbet is a game where you practise social skills on someone who actually reacts to you.

The person across from you is played by an AI, and it is not trying to be helpful. Ask closed questions and it gets bored. Talk about yourself too much and it loses interest. Ask the right thing at the right moment and it opens up. The interest bar at the top of the screen shows this as it happens — if it reaches zero, the conversation is over.

WHEN THE CONVERSATION ENDS
The assessment does not open with praise. It goes turn by turn: what you did, why that move worked or didn't, and what you could have said instead. No general advice like "be more open" — every note is tied to a turn number and to a sentence you actually wrote. The suggested lines are lines you could really use.

The scoring is harsh. An average conversation gets 50-60; above 80 means it was genuinely good. An assessor that praises everything teaches nothing.

SPEAK INSTEAD OF TYPING
You can talk instead of writing. The character's voice is male or female depending on the scenario. Your pace and your pauses go into the assessment too — the way they would in a real conversation.

16 SCENARIOS
The next table at a cafe, a house party, a work dinner, sitting down next to someone you don't know, interrupting someone politely, turning down an invitation. They open up from easy to hard. You can replay a scenario against a different personality — the same scene, a much harder person.

SCENARIOS BUILT FROM YOUR GAPS
After a few conversations it becomes clear which skill is lagging. One tap generates a new scenario aimed at exactly that skill.

HISTORY
Every conversation is kept with its transcript and its assessment. You can see your repeating habits — whether you always stall in the same place, or whether you are getting better.

PRIVACY
No account, no email. Your transcripts and assessments are stored on your phone. During a conversation the text passes through our server to the AI model that writes the character's replies and your assessment, and your voice goes to a speech recognition service to be turned into text. Our server does not keep the text, with two exceptions: a request that fails is kept for 7 days so we can fix the fault, and, only if you switch it on in Settings, a small sample of conversations is kept for 90 days to improve the app. Neither is linked to you. Audio is never saved as a file.

SUBSCRIPTION
Two conversations are included without a subscription — with the live character, voice mode and the full assessment. Sohbet Pro is monthly and starts with a one-week trial: it removes that limit and also unlocks the coach report, which reads all of your conversations together, and scenario generation aimed at your gaps.

The subscription renews itself at the end of each period. You can cancel any time under Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions.
