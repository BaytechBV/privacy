Support – GaLaBau Pflanzenkenntnis

Questions or problems? Email utqsall@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Which exam does the app cover?

Practice for the Zwischen- and Abschlussprüfung as Gärtner/in Garten- und Landschaftsbau in Germany, the LAP Garten- und Grünflächengestaltung in Austria and the QV as Gärtner/in EFZ in Switzerland. All questions and drawings are written by us, not original exam questions; photos come from free sources with licence notes.

Can my training company buy it for me?

The full version is a one-time purchase with Family Sharing; parents or a training company can buy it on their Apple ID and share it via Family Sharing.

How do I restore the full version?

Settings › Restore purchases, with the same Apple ID.

Where is my data?

On your device (and in your private iCloud if sync is on). There is no account and no server of ours.
