Privacy Policy – Pfändix

2026-11-24

Summary

Pfändix has no account and no server of ours. Your data stays on your device. The only data that can reach us is anonymous usage statistics, which you can switch off.

Your data

Your calculations, monthly entries and PDF summaries are stored only on your device. There is no cloud sync. Nothing is uploaded to us. Deleting the app, or Settings › Alle Eingaben löschen, removes them.

Permissions

Notifications (optional): one local reminder a year when the official garnishment table changes on 1 July; it contains no amounts or names. Pfändix uses no camera, microphone, location, contacts or health data.

Usage statistics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase (without the advertising identifier), provided by Google Ireland Ltd. It collects which screens and buttons are used, in-app purchase events, the approximate country/region Google derives from the IP address, and basic technical data (app and iOS version, device model). Events never contain incomes, amounts or the number of dependants. The data is not linked to your identity and not used for tracking or advertising; Google processes it for us and keeps it for at most 14 months. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); Google may process data in the USA under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. See Google's Privacy Policy. Switch it off in the app: Settings › Usage statistics.

Purchases

Pfändix Plus is a one-time purchase handled by Apple via the App Store. We receive no payment details; the app only asks Apple whether the purchase is active. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Independence

Pfändix is an independent calculation aid and not legal advice. It is not affiliated with any court, bank or debt counselling service; the decision of the court or the bank prevails.

Your rights and contact

You can delete any item in the app at any time; deleting the app removes everything stored on the device. You can object to the usage statistics at any time by switching them off (Art. 21 GDPR). Questions: utqsall@gmail.com. Controller: Mustafa Utku Bayık (BaytechBV).
