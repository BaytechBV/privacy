Support – Pfändix

Questions or problems? Email utqsall@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Which table does Pfändix use?

The garnishment exemption limits valid from 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027 (§850c ZPO) and the Austrian subsistence minimum table; Pfändix reminds you when a new table applies.

Is my income data sent anywhere?

No. All entries stay on your device.

How do I restore or cancel Plus?

Restore: Settings › Restore purchases, with the same Apple ID.

Where is my data?

On your device only. There is no account and no server of ours.
