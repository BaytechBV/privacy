Privacy Policy – Kunskapsprov Tandläkare

2026-11-26

Summary

Kunskapsprov Tandläkare has no account and no server of ours. Your data stays on your device and in your private iCloud. The only data that can reach us is anonymous usage statistics, which you can switch off.

Your data

Your learning progress, answers, open mistakes, patient-case runs, exam-simulation results, saved terms and your exam date are stored on your device and in your private iCloud (Apple CloudKit), so they sync between your own devices. Free-text MEQ answers never leave your device except through your own iCloud. Nothing is uploaded to us. Deleting the app deletes the data on that device; iCloud data can be removed in the iOS Settings app.

Permissions

Notifications (optional): a local daily study reminder and a reminder before your exam date, scheduled on the device; no push server. The app uses no camera, microphone, location, photos or health data.

Usage statistics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase (without the advertising identifier), provided by Google Ireland Ltd. It collects which screens and buttons are used, in-app purchase events, the approximate country/region Google derives from the IP address, and basic technical data (app and iOS version, device model). Events never contain your answers or any text you enter. The data is not linked to your identity and not used for tracking or advertising; Google processes it for us and keeps it for at most 14 months. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); Google may process data in the USA under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. See Google's Privacy Policy. Switch it off in the app: Settings › Usage statistics.

Purchases

The full version is a one-time purchase handled by Apple via the App Store. We receive no payment details; the app only asks Apple whether the purchase is active. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Independence

Kunskapsprov Tandläkare is an independent study app and is not affiliated with Socialstyrelsen or any university. Questions and cases are our own and are not taken from past papers. It is study material and does not replace clinical judgement. The exam format is set by the exam organiser.

Your rights and contact

You can delete any item in the app at any time; deleting the app removes everything stored on the device. You can object to the usage statistics at any time by switching them off (Art. 21 GDPR). Questions: utqsall@gmail.com. Controller: Mustafa Utku Bayık (BaytechBV).
