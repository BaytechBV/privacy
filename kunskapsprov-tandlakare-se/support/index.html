Support – Kunskapsprov Tandläkare

Questions or problems? Email utqsall@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Which exam does the app cover?

The Swedish knowledge test (kunskapsprov) for dentists educated outside the EU/EEA: the theoretical part (patient cases, MEQ) and the practical part (OSCE talks, phantom drills). Questions and cases are written by us, not past exam questions.

Is everything in English too?

Yes. Every question, case and term is available in Swedish and English; switch in Settings.

How do I restore the full version?

Settings › Restore purchases, with the same Apple ID. Family Sharing is on.

Where is my data?

On your device and in your private iCloud. There is no account and no server of ours.
