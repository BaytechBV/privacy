Support – Vaporis

Questions or problems? Email utqsall@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Which method does Vaporis use?

The steady-state Glaser method (DIN 4108-3 style): temperature profile, saturation and vapour pressure across the layers, condensate mass over 90 winter days and drying balance. Capillary transport and sorption are not modelled.

Where do the material values come from?

Typical λ and μ values from textbooks and data sheets, stated as facts. You can enter values from your own product data sheet.

How do I restore or cancel Plus?

Restore: Settings › Restore purchases, with the same Apple ID.

Where is my data?

On your device and in your private iCloud. There is no account and no server of ours.
