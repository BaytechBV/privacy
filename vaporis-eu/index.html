Privacy Policy – Vaporis

2026-11-23

Summary

Vaporis has no account and no server of ours. Your data stays on your device and in your private iCloud. The only data that can reach us is anonymous usage statistics, which you can switch off.

Your data

Your projects, wall and roof build-ups, your own material values, variants and PDF reports are stored on your device and in your private iCloud (Apple CloudKit), so they sync between your own devices. Nothing is uploaded to us. Deleting the app deletes the data on that device; iCloud data can be removed in the iOS Settings app.

Permissions

Location (optional, only while in use): only for the optional local-climate check; Vaporis asks Apple WeatherKit for your place's winter temperatures. The location is not stored and not sent to us. Weather data: Apple Weather. Vaporis uses no camera, microphone, contacts or health data.

Usage statistics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase (without the advertising identifier), provided by Google Ireland Ltd. It collects which screens and buttons are used, in-app purchase events, the approximate country/region Google derives from the IP address, and basic technical data (app and iOS version, device model). The data is not linked to your identity and not used for tracking or advertising; Google processes it for us and keeps it for at most 14 months. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); Google may process data in the USA under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. See Google's Privacy Policy. Switch it off in the app: Settings › Usage statistics.

Purchases

Vaporis Plus is a one-time purchase handled by Apple via the App Store. We receive no payment details; the app only asks Apple whether the purchase is active. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Independence

Vaporis is an independent pre-design tool using the steady-state Glaser method. It does not replace professional design or a formal proof.

Your rights and contact

You can delete any item in the app at any time; deleting the app removes everything stored on the device. You can object to the usage statistics at any time by switching them off (Art. 21 GDPR). Questions: utqsall@gmail.com. Controller: Mustafa Utku Bayık (BaytechBV).
