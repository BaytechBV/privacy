Scanfield – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Summary

Scanfield collects no data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDK. We run no server for the app.

Microphone and speech recognition

During a drill the app listens for one short word (a colour, left/right or a number). Speech recognition runs on the iPhone itself (Apple on-device recognition); the app never falls back to server recognition. Audio is kept only in memory for a moment and is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere. Without microphone permission the app works in touch mode.

What stays on the device

Profiles (nickname, age, strong foot, answer language) and drill results (recognised word, correct or wrong, reaction time) are stored only on this iPhone so the app can show results and progress. There is no iCloud sync. Deleting the app deletes everything. We cannot see any of it.

Children

Scanfield is used by young footballers together with a parent or coach. It is not in the Kids category. Purchases, settings and links that leave the app are behind a parent gate.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We receive no payment details or personal information; the app only asks Apple whether Plus is active.
