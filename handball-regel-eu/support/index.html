Support – Handballregeln

Questions or problems? Email utqsall@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Which test does the app cover?

Practice for the handball referee rules test: for referee candidates, active referees before the season and table officials. All 18 IHF rules, with the rule changes from 1 July 2025. Questions are written by us, not official test questions.

Can I switch between German and Danish?

Yes. Settings › Language. Both languages have the same questions and your progress is kept.

How do I restore the full version?

Settings › Restore purchases, with the same Apple ID. Family Sharing is on.

Where is my data?

On your device and in your private iCloud. There is no account and no server of ours.
