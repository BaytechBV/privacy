Hvilke data appen behandler

Appen kræver ingen konto. Dine fremskridt, indstillinger og prøveresultater gemmes kun på din enhed. Det eneste, der forlader din enhed, er den anonyme brugsstatistik, der er beskrevet nedenfor.

Brugsstatistik (Google Analytics for Firebase)

For at se, hvilke dele af appen der bliver brugt, og hvor den kan blive bedre, sender appen anonyme brugshændelser til Google Analytics for Firebase (Google Ireland Ltd., Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Irland): hvilke skærme du åbner, hvilke knapper du trykker på, hvornår en øverunde eller prøveeksamen starter og slutter (med resultatet som et interval, fx “70–84 %”) og trin i et køb. Firebase-SDK'et registrerer også tekniske data som appversion, iOS-version, enhedsmodel, land/region (udledt af IP-adressen, som ikke gemmes) og et tilfældigt app-instans-id, der nulstilles, når du sletter appen. Navn, e-mailadresse, spørgsmåls- eller svartekster og andet indhold, du indtaster, sendes ikke. Dataene kobles ikke til din identitet. Appen bruger ikke reklame-id'et (IDFA), viser ingen reklamer og sporer dig ikke på tværs af apps eller websteder. Retsgrundlag: vores legitime interesse i at forbedre appen (GDPR art. 6, stk. 1, litra f). Google kan behandle data i USA; Google LLC er certificeret under EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Hændelsesdata slettes efter 2 måneder. Se Googles privatlivspolitik og Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Køb

Engangskøbet i appen håndteres helt af Apple via App Store. Vi modtager ikke dine betalingsoplysninger. Se Apples privatlivspolitik.

Bedømmelser

Appen kan bede dig om at bedømme den med Apples almindelige dialog. Bedømmelser håndteres af Apple.

Slet dine data

Når du sletter appen, slettes alle data, den har gemt på din enhed. Under Indstillinger kan du også nulstille dine fremskridt.

Dine rettigheder

Har du spørgsmål om denne politik eller dine data (GDPR art. 15–21), så kontakt os på adressen ovenfor. Du kan altid slå brugsstatistikken fra i appens indstillinger (“Send brugsstatistik”); derefter sendes ingen hændelser (GDPR art. 21). Når du sletter appen, slettes app-instans-id'et også. Da statistikken ikke er koblet til din identitet, kan vi ikke finde dine data i den.

Ændringer

Vi kan ændre denne politik. Datoen ovenfor viser den gældende version.

Privacy Policy – Duelighedsbevis – Teori & Test

Last updated: 2026-10-14

Who we are

This app is published by Mustafa Utku Bayık. Contact: utqsall@gmail.com.

What data the app processes

The app does not require an account. Your learning progress, settings and exam results are stored only on your device. The only data that leaves your device is the anonymous usage statistics described below.

Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To find out which parts of the app are used and where it can be improved, the app sends anonymous usage events to Google Analytics for Firebase (Google Ireland Ltd., Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland): which screens you open, which buttons you tap, when a practice round or mock exam starts and ends (with the score as a range, e.g. “70–84%”), and purchase steps. The Firebase SDK also records technical data such as app version, iOS version, device model, country/region (derived from the IP address, which is not stored) and a random app-instance ID that is reset when you delete the app. No name, e-mail address, question or answer text, or other content you enter is sent. The data is not linked to your identity. The app does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA), shows no ads and does not track you across apps or websites. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Google may process data in the USA; Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Event data is deleted after 2 months. See Google's Privacy Policy and Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Purchases

The one-time in-app purchase is handled entirely by Apple via the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Ratings

The app may ask you to rate it using Apple's standard rating dialog. Ratings are handled by Apple.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all data it stored on your device.

Your rights

If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us at the address above. You can switch the usage statistics off at any time in the app's settings (“Send usage statistics”); from then on no events are sent (Art. 21 GDPR). Deleting the app also deletes the app-instance ID. Because the statistics are not linked to your identity, we cannot identify your data in them.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
