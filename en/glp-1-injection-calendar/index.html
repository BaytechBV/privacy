Frequently asked questions

Does this tool tell me which dose to take?

No. It gives no dose, titration or missed-dose advice. It only puts the schedule you type in onto dates. Your doctor or pharmacist decides your dose.

Which medicines can I use it with?

Any weekly or daily injection your doctor prescribed, for example Wegovy®, Ozempic®, Mounjaro®, Saxenda®, Trulicity® or Victoza®. Not affiliated with any manufacturer; trademarks belong to their owners.

What if I missed an injection?

Read the patient information leaflet and ask your doctor or pharmacist. This calendar cannot advise on missed doses.

Is my information saved or sent?

No. Everything is calculated in your browser; nothing is stored or sent.
