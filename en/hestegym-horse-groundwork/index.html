About the app

Hestegym is the training programme for everyone who works their horse from the ground: exercise library, week plan, timer and log in one place.

  • Exercise library by goal: topline, hindquarters, balance and suppleness
  • Every exercise with a drawing, step by step and the number of repetitions
  • Timer for hold times and repetitions during the session
  • Week plan with sessions, rest days and riding
  • Horse profile with goals, limitations and a vet note
  • Log of every session and how your horse felt
  • Progress per goal and reminders before the session
  • Works offline
  • In English, Norwegian, Danish, Swedish, Finnish, German and Dutch

Today's session with the timer, 12 exercises and the log can be used without a purchase. The full version is a one-time purchase that unlocks the whole library, week plans, reminders, progress and more horses. No subscription.

Hestegym does not give veterinary advice and does not replace a vet or physiotherapist. If your horse is injured, lame or under treatment, ask your vet before you start.
