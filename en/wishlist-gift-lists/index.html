About the app

The wish list the whole family sees – no more getting the same gift twice.

Make one list per occasion, share it with a link and let the family reserve in secret. Grandma sees what is left, your cousin sees what is taken, and the person who made the wishes never sees what was bought.

ADD WISHES FAST
• Paste a link from any shop – title and image are fetched once
• Share straight from Safari or a shop app: “Add to wish list”
• Your own photo or just text
• Price, colour/size, quantity, how much (one to three stars), “more than one is fine” and a note to the family

SHARE WITH THE FAMILY
• Invite via Messages, WhatsApp or e-mail – iCloud sharing, no account needed
• Those with the app see the list live and tap “I'm buying”
• The list owner does not see reservations: the surprise stays
• If two people reserve the same thing, the first one keeps it
• Without the app: send the list as an image or text

FAMILY AND TO BUY
• All lists shared with you, grouped by person, with the next date
• Everything you are buying in one place: reserved, bought, wrapped, given
• Budget per person

OCCASIONS AND REMINDERS
• Christmas, birthdays, name days, weddings, graduations, christenings, confirmations and your own dates
• A reminder 14 days before – with how many wishes are left

SECRET SANTA
• Add participants, pairs who can't draw each other, budget and date
• The draw happens on the phone and nobody draws themselves
• Everyone gets their result as a private link that opens even without the app – the organiser doesn't see the draw in the app
• Or pass the phone around and reveal one at a time

CHILDREN'S LISTS
• Make lists for your children – only a first name, no account for the child

PURCHASE
3 own lists and everything about giving (sharing, reserving, To buy, budget) work without a purchase, and so does a Secret Santa draw for up to 5 people. One purchase unlocks full access: unlimited lists, more children's lists, Secret Santa for bigger groups, gift ideas and PDF export. The purchase covers the whole family through Family Sharing. No subscription, no ads.

PRIVACY
Lists are stored on your devices and in your iCloud. The app sends anonymous usage statistics through Google Analytics for Firebase (e.g. which screens and buttons are used, in-app purchases and the approximate country); you can switch this off in Settings.
