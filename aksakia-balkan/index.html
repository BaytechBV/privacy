Πολιτική απορρήτου – Aksakia

Τελευταία ενημέρωση: 2026-11-14

Με λίγα λόγια

Το Aksakia δεν έχει λογαριασμό ούτε δικό μας διακομιστή. Ό,τι καταχωρείς μένει στο iPhone σου. Τα μόνα δεδομένα που φτάνουν σε εμάς είναι ανώνυμα στατιστικά χρήσης, τα οποία μπορείς να απενεργοποιήσεις.

Τα δεδομένα σου

Το Aksakia δεν ζητά καμία άδεια: ούτε μικρόφωνο, ούτε τοποθεσία, ούτε κάμερα, ούτε ειδοποιήσεις. Η μηχανή ρυθμού λειτουργεί εξ ολοκλήρου στο iPhone σου· δεν γίνεται καμία ηχογράφηση. Οι δικοί σου ρυθμοί, οι λίστες, τα αγαπημένα και το ημερολόγιο εξάσκησης μένουν μόνο στο iPhone σου. Οι ρυθμίσεις καθυστέρησης για ακουστικά Bluetooth μένουν στη συσκευή.

Αναπαραγωγή στο παρασκήνιο

Όσο παίζει ένας ρυθμός, το Aksakia συνεχίζει να παίζει στο παρασκήνιο και δείχνει τον χορό και το τέμπο στην οθόνη κλειδώματος (Now Playing), όπου μπορείς να κάνεις παύση ή να πας στον επόμενο· όταν δεν παίζει τίποτα, ο ήχος στο παρασκήνιο δεν χρησιμοποιείται. Οι ρυθμοί και οι ήχοι είναι ενσωματωμένοι στην εφαρμογή και λειτουργούν χωρίς σύνδεση.

Στατιστικά χρήσης

Χρησιμοποιούμε το Google Analytics for Firebase (FirebaseAnalyticsCore, χωρίς αναγνωριστικό διαφήμισης) της Google Ireland Ltd. Συλλέγει: ένα τυχαίο αναγνωριστικό εγκατάστασης που δημιουργεί η εφαρμογή (όχι το αναγνωριστικό διαφήμισης), ποιες οθόνες και ποια κουμπιά χρησιμοποιούνται και τα βήματα στην εφαρμογή (για παράδειγμα ρυθμός από τη βιβλιοθήκη ξεκίνησε, εκπαιδευτής τέμπο ξεκίνησε, λίστα ξεκίνησε, σετ ήχων άλλαξε), συμβάντα αγορών εντός εφαρμογής (προϊόν και τιμή), την κατά προσέγγιση τοποθεσία (χώρα/περιοχή) που υπολογίζει η Google από τη διεύθυνση IP και τεχνικά στοιχεία εγκατάστασης (Firebase Installations). Τα συμβάντα δεν περιέχουν ποτέ ήχο (η εφαρμογή δεν ηχογραφεί τίποτα), ονόματα των δικών σου ρυθμών ή λιστών ή άλλο κείμενο που πληκτρολογείς. Τα δεδομένα δεν συνδέονται με εσένα και δεν χρησιμοποιούνται για παρακολούθηση ή διαφήμιση· η Google τα επεξεργάζεται για λογαριασμό μας και τα κρατά το πολύ 14 μήνες. Νομική βάση: το έννομο συμφέρον μας να βελτιώνουμε την εφαρμογή (άρθρο 6 παρ. 1 στ. στ' ΓΚΠΔ)· η Google μπορεί να επεξεργάζεται δεδομένα στις ΗΠΑ βάσει του πλαισίου EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Απενεργοποίηση στην εφαρμογή: Ρυθμίσεις › Αποστολή στατιστικών χρήσης.

Politica de confidențialitate – Aksakia

Ultima actualizare: 2026-11-14

Pe scurt

Aksakia nu are cont și nici server al nostru. Ce introduci rămâne pe iPhone-ul tău. Singurele date care ajung la noi sunt statistici anonime de utilizare, pe care le poți opri.

Datele tale

Aksakia nu cere nicio permisiune: nici microfon, nici locație, nici cameră, nici notificări. Motorul de ritm rulează în întregime pe iPhone-ul tău; nu se înregistrează nimic. Ritmurile tale, listele, favoritele și jurnalul de exersare rămân doar pe iPhone-ul tău. Setările de întârziere pentru căștile Bluetooth rămân pe dispozitiv.

Redare în fundal

Cât timp cântă un ritm, Aksakia continuă redarea în fundal și arată dansul și tempoul pe ecranul de blocare (Now Playing), unde poți pune pauză sau trece mai departe; când nu cântă nimic, sunetul în fundal nu este folosit. Ritmurile și sunetele sunt incluse în aplicație și funcționează offline.

Statistici de utilizare

Folosim Google Analytics for Firebase (FirebaseAnalyticsCore, fără identificatorul de publicitate), furnizat de Google Ireland Ltd. Colectează: un ID aleatoriu de instalare creat de aplicație (nu ID-ul de publicitate), ce ecrane și butoane sunt folosite și pașii din aplicație (de exemplu ritm pornit din bibliotecă, antrenor de tempo pornit, listă pornită, set de sunete schimbat), evenimentele de cumpărare din aplicație (produs și preț), locația aproximativă (țară/regiune) dedusă de Google din adresa IP și date tehnice de instalare (Firebase Installations). Evenimentele nu conțin niciodată sunet (aplicația nu înregistrează nimic), numele ritmurilor sau listelor tale sau alt text pe care îl introduci. Datele nu sunt legate de tine și nu sunt folosite pentru urmărire sau publicitate; Google le prelucrează pentru noi și le păstrează cel mult 14 luni. Temei juridic: interesul nostru legitim de a îmbunătăți aplicația (art. 6 alin. (1) lit. f) GDPR); Google poate prelucra date în SUA în baza Cadrului UE-SUA privind protecția datelor. Oprire în aplicație: Setări › Trimite statistici de utilizare.

Cumpărări

Cumpărările sunt gestionate de Apple. Nu primim date de plată; aplicația doar întreabă Apple dacă achiziția este deblocată.

Notificări

Aksakia nu trimite notificări.

Drepturile tale și contact

Dacă ștergi o înregistrare în aplicație, aceasta dispare; dacă ștergi aplicația, dispare tot ce era salvat pe iPhone. Te poți opune oricând statisticilor de utilizare oprindu-le (art. 21 GDPR). Întrebări: utqsall@gmail.com. Operator: Mustafa Utku Bayık (BaytechBV).

Privacy Policy – Aksakia

Last updated: 2026-11-14

Summary

Aksakia has no account and no server of ours. What you enter stays on your iPhone. The only data that reaches us is anonymous usage statistics, which you can switch off.

Your data

Aksakia asks for no permissions: no microphone, no location, no camera, no notifications. The rhythm engine runs entirely on your iPhone; nothing is recorded. Your own rhythms, setlists, favourites and practice log stay on your iPhone only. Latency settings for Bluetooth headphones stay on the device.

Background playback

While a rhythm is playing, Aksakia keeps playing in the background and shows the dance and tempo on the Lock Screen (Now Playing), where you can pause or skip; when nothing is playing, background audio is not used. Rhythms and sounds are built into the app and work offline.

Usage statistics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase (FirebaseAnalyticsCore, without the advertising identifier), provided by Google Ireland Ltd. It collects: a random app instance ID created by the app (not the advertising ID), which screens and buttons are used and the steps in the app (for example rhythm played from the library, tempo trainer started, setlist started, sound set changed), in-app purchase events (product and price), the approximate location (country/region) that Google derives from the IP address, and technical installation data (Firebase Installations). Events never contain audio (the app records none), names of your own rhythms or setlists, or other text you enter. The data is not linked to you and not used for tracking or advertising; Google processes it for us and keeps it for at most 14 months. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); Google may process data in the USA under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Switch it off in the app: Settings › Send usage statistics.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. We receive no payment details; the app only asks Apple whether the purchase is unlocked.

Notifications

Aksakia sends no notifications.

Your rights and contact

Deleting an entry in the app removes it; deleting the app removes everything stored on your iPhone. You can object to the usage statistics at any time by switching them off (Art. 21 GDPR). Questions: utqsall@gmail.com. Controller: Mustafa Utku Bayık (BaytechBV).
