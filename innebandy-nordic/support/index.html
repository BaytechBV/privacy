Questions or problems? Email utqsall@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Which test does the app cover?

Practice for the floorball referee rules test in Sweden and Finland (new referees on the referee course and licensed referees before the season), based on the IFF Rules of the Game valid from 1 July 2026. All questions, situations and drawings are written by us; the rule text is not quoted.

Swedish or Finnish?

Both languages contain the same questions; switch in Settings › Language. Your progress is kept.

How do I restore the full version?

Settings › Restore purchases, with the same Apple ID. The full version is a one-time purchase with Family Sharing.

Where is my data?

On your device and in your private iCloud. There is no account and no server of ours.
