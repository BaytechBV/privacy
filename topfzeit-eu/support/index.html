Support – Topfzeit

Questions or problems? Email utqsall@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Does Topfzeit know my product?

No. You enter mixing ratio, pot life, consumption and recoat times from your own technical data sheet. Topfzeit calculates with those values.

How is the dew point check done?

Magnus formula from air temperature and relative humidity; the surface must be at least 3 K above the dew point.

How do I restore or cancel Plus?

Restore: Settings › Restore purchases, with the same Apple ID. Cancel: Settings app › your name › Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before renewal.

Where is my data?

On your device and in your private iCloud. There is no account and no server of ours.
