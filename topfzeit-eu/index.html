Privacy Policy – Topfzeit

2026-11-23

Summary

Topfzeit has no account and no server of ours. Your data stays on your device and in your private iCloud. The only data that can reach us is anonymous usage statistics, which you can switch off.

Your data

Your products you enter from data sheets, sites, mixes, measurements, photos and condition reports (PDF) are stored on your device and in your private iCloud (Apple CloudKit), so they sync between your own devices. If you share an item through iCloud, only the people you invite can see it. Nothing is uploaded to us. Deleting the app deletes the data on that device; iCloud data can be removed in the iOS Settings app.

Permissions

Location (optional, only while in use): to load the outdoor weather of the site from Apple WeatherKit; the location stays on the device and is not sent to us. Weather data: Apple Weather. Camera (optional): to read a mixing ratio from a product label (Live Text, the image is not stored) or to take a photo for the report. Notifications (optional): local timers for pot life and recoat windows; no push server.

Usage statistics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase (without the advertising identifier), provided by Google Ireland Ltd. It collects which screens and buttons are used, in-app purchase events, the approximate country/region Google derives from the IP address, and basic technical data (app and iOS version, device model). Events never contain site names, places, batch numbers, measurements or photos. The data is not linked to your identity and not used for tracking or advertising; Google processes it for us and keeps it for at most 14 months. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); Google may process data in the USA under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. See Google's Privacy Policy. Switch it off in the app: Settings › Usage statistics.

Purchases

Topfzeit Plus is an auto-renewable subscription handled by Apple via the App Store. We receive no payment details; the app only asks Apple whether the purchase is active. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Independence

Topfzeit is an independent tool and contains no manufacturer data; the product's technical data sheet always prevails.

Your rights and contact

You can delete any item in the app at any time; deleting the app removes everything stored on the device. You can object to the usage statistics at any time by switching them off (Art. 21 GDPR). Questions: utqsall@gmail.com. Controller: Mustafa Utku Bayık (BaytechBV).
