English

Båtkörkort – Förarintyg 2026 is a practice app for the Swedish leisure boat certificate (Förarintyg för fritidsbåt), published by Mustafa Utku Bayık (BaytechBV).

  • Stored on your device only: your answers, mistakes, exam results, chosen exam date and whether the full version is unlocked. No account, no login, no server of our own. Deleting the app deletes the data.
  • Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase): the app sends anonymous events about which screens and buttons are used and how practice goes (e.g. "practice started", right/wrong, score band). No question text, no answers you type, no names, no e-mail, no advertising ID (IDFA) and no tracking across apps or websites. The data is not linked to you and is only used to improve the app. Events are kept for at most 14 months and then deleted. Google LLC acts as processor.
  • Turn statistics off at any time: Settings → Privacy → "Send usage statistics".
  • Purchases are handled by Apple (App Store). We receive no payment details.
  • No ads, no other third-party services.

Questions: see support.
