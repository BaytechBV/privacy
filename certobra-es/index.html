Privacy Policy – Certobra

2026-11-25

Summary

Certobra has no account and no server of ours. Your data stays on your device. The only data that can reach us is anonymous usage statistics, which you can switch off.

Your data

Your BC3 budgets you open, chapters and items, measurement lines, sketches, certifications and PDF reports are stored only on your device. There is no cloud sync; you share files yourself (BC3 or PDF) through the iOS share sheet, Files, Mail or AirDrop. Nothing is uploaded to us. Deleting the app deletes the data.

Permissions

Camera (optional): only when you tap Photo to add a sketch to a measurement line; the photo is stored with the project on your device. Certobra uses no location, microphone, contacts, notifications or health data.

Usage statistics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase (without the advertising identifier), provided by Google Ireland Ltd. It collects which screens and buttons are used, in-app purchase events, the approximate country/region Google derives from the IP address, and basic technical data (app and iOS version, device model). Events never contain project names, addresses, amounts or measurements. The data is not linked to your identity and not used for tracking or advertising; Google processes it for us and keeps it for at most 14 months. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); Google may process data in the USA under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. See Google's Privacy Policy. Switch it off in the app: Settings › Usage statistics.

Purchases

Certobra Plus is an auto-renewable subscription handled by Apple via the App Store. We receive no payment details; the app only asks Apple whether the purchase is active. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Independence

Certobra is an independent tool for the open FIEBDC-3 (BC3) format. It is not affiliated with FIEBDC or any software vendor; check every certification before you sign it.

Your rights and contact

You can delete any item in the app at any time; deleting the app removes everything stored on the device. You can object to the usage statistics at any time by switching them off (Art. 21 GDPR). Questions: utqsall@gmail.com. Controller: Mustafa Utku Bayık (BaytechBV).
