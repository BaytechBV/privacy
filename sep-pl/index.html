Privacy Policy – Uprawnienia G1

2026-11-24

Summary

Uprawnienia G1 has no account and no server of ours. Your data stays on your device. The only data that can reach us is anonymous usage statistics, which you can switch off.

Your data

Your learning progress, answers, open mistakes, mock-exam results, self-ratings and practice-task results are stored only on your device; there is no account, no server and no cloud sync. Nothing is uploaded to us. Deleting the app (or “Wyzeruj postęp” in Settings) deletes this data.

Permissions

Notifications (optional): a local question of the day in the evening and a reminder before your exam date, scheduled on the device; no push server. Uprawnienia G1 uses no camera, microphone, location, photos or health data. Oral answers are not recorded: you say them aloud yourself and rate your answer.

Usage statistics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase (without the advertising identifier), provided by Google Ireland Ltd. It collects which screens and buttons are used, in-app purchase events, the approximate country/region Google derives from the IP address, and basic technical data (app and iOS version, device model). Events never contain your answers, self-ratings or exam date. The data is not linked to your identity and not used for tracking or advertising; Google processes it for us and keeps it for at most 14 months. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); Google may process data in the USA under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. See Google's Privacy Policy. Switch it off in the app: Settings › Usage statistics.

Purchases

Uprawnienia G1 Plus is a one-time purchase handled by Apple via the App Store. We receive no payment details; the app only asks Apple whether the purchase is active. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Independence

Uprawnienia G1 is an independent learning tool and is not affiliated with SEP, URE, any qualification commission or training provider. The current regulations, standards and the examining commission always prevail.

Your rights and contact

You can delete any item in the app at any time; deleting the app removes everything stored on the device. You can object to the usage statistics at any time by switching them off (Art. 21 GDPR). Questions: utqsall@gmail.com. Controller: Mustafa Utku Bayık (BaytechBV).
