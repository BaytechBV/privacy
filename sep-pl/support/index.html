Support – Uprawnienia G1

Questions or problems? Email utqsall@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Which exam does the app cover?

Practice for the Polish G1 electrical qualification exam (group 1, operation E and supervision D), with questions, wiring diagrams and measurement tasks written by us and checked against the published exam scope.

Does the app replace a course?

No. It is for practice before the exam. The exam itself takes place before a qualification commission.

How do I restore or cancel Plus?

Restore: Settings › Restore purchases, with the same Apple ID.

Where is my data?

On your device and in your private iCloud. There is no account and no server of ours.
