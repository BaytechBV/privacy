Support – Lesegut

Questions or problems? Email utqsall@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Which rules does Lesegut use?

EU wine rules and the German and Austrian national rules for enrichment and sweetening by wine-growing zone; French and Italian registers follow in a later version.

Can the cellar master see the same book?

Yes, with Plus you can share the cellar book through iCloud with people you invite.

How do I restore or cancel Plus?

Restore: Settings › Restore purchases, with the same Apple ID. Cancel: Settings app › your name › Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before renewal.

Where is my data?

On your device and in your private iCloud. There is no account and no server of ours.
