Privacy Policy – VCA Examen – Basis & VOL

Last updated: 2026-10-11

Who we are

This app is published by Mustafa Utku Bayık. Contact: utqsall@gmail.com.

What data the app processes

The app does not require an account and does not collect, store on servers or share any personal data. Your learning progress, settings and exam results are stored only on your device.

No tracking, analytics or ads

The app contains no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites.

Purchases

The one-time in-app purchase is handled entirely by Apple via the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Ratings

The app may ask you to rate it using Apple's standard rating dialog. Ratings are handled by Apple.

Read aloud

The read-aloud feature uses the on-device iOS speech engine. No audio is recorded or sent anywhere.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all data it stored on your device.

Your rights

If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us at the address above. Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete.

Changes
