Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Vaktplan ("Vaktplan – Shifts & Pay" on the App Store) is made by Baytech BV / Mustafa Utku Bayık.

The app has no account, no server of ours and no advertising. Nothing you enter (shifts, notes, wages, rules) leaves your device through us. The only data that leaves the device is the anonymous usage statistics described below.

Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase): to learn which parts of the app are used, the app sends anonymous usage events to Google Analytics for Firebase (Google Ireland Ltd., Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland): which screens you open, which buttons you tap and purchase steps. The Firebase SDK also records technical data such as app and iOS version, device model, country/region (derived from the IP address, which is not stored) and a random app-instance ID that is reset when you delete the app. Never your shifts, notes, wages, pay rules or any text you type; the data is not linked to your identity. No advertising identifier (IDFA), no tracking across apps or websites. Legal basis: legitimate interest in improving the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Google may process data in the USA (EU-US Data Privacy Framework). Event data is deleted after 2 months. You can switch this off at any time in Settings (“Send usage statistics”). See Google's Privacy Policy.

Your shift types, shifts, notes, wage, supplement rules and settings are stored only on your iPhone (in the app's own storage). The home-screen widget reads a short summary (next shifts, hours this month) from a storage area shared only between the app and its widget on your device (App Group).

Calendar: if you choose “Add shifts to calendar”, the app asks for write-only access and adds events to the calendar you pick. It cannot read your calendar. Notifications: shift reminders are scheduled locally on the device.

Files you share (.ics or an image of your month) are sent only where you send them, using the iOS share sheet.

Purchases are handled by Apple. We receive no payment or personal details; the app only checks on the device whether Pro is active.

Deleting the app deletes all its data. Apple's backups (iCloud/computer) may contain the app's data under your own Apple account.

The app is not directed at children under 13. Questions: open the support page linked on the App Store.
