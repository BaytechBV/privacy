Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Vaktplan ("Shift Planner – Rota & Pay") is made by Baytech BV / Mustafa Utku Bayık.

We collect no data. The app has no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. Nothing you enter leaves your device through us.

Your shift types, shifts, notes, wage, supplement rules and settings are stored only on your iPhone (in the app's own storage). The home-screen widget reads a short summary (next shifts, hours this month) from a storage area shared only between the app and its widget on your device (App Group).

Calendar: if you choose “Add shifts to calendar”, the app asks for write-only access and adds events to the calendar you pick. It cannot read your calendar. Notifications: shift reminders are scheduled locally on the device.

Files you share (.ics or an image of your month) are sent only where you send them, using the iOS share sheet.

Purchases are handled by Apple. We receive no payment or personal details; the app only checks on the device whether Pro is active.

Deleting the app deletes all its data. Apple's backups (iCloud/computer) may contain the app's data under your own Apple account.

The app is not directed at children under 13. Questions: open the support page linked on the App Store.
