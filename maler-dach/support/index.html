Support – Maler Prüfung

Questions or problems? Email utqsall@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Which exam does the app cover?

Practice for the Gesellenprüfung Teil 1 and Teil 2 as Maler/in und Lackierer/in in Germany, the Lehrabschlussprüfung (LAP) in Austria and the Qualifikationsverfahren (QV) as Malerin/Maler EFZ in Switzerland. All questions, tasks, sketches and colour examples are written by us, not original exam questions.

Can my training company buy it for me?

The full version is a one-time purchase with Family Sharing; parents or a training company can buy it on their Apple ID and share it via Family Sharing.

How do I restore the full version?

Settings › Restore purchases, with the same Apple ID.

Where is my data?

On your device and in your private iCloud. There is no account and no server of ours.
