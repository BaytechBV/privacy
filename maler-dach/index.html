Privacy Policy – Maler Prüfung

2026-11-27

Summary

Maler Prüfung has no account and no server of ours. Your data stays on your device and in your private iCloud. Anonymous usage statistics are switched off by default; only if you switch them on can anonymous usage data reach us.

Data on your device and in iCloud

Your learning progress, answers, open mistakes, measurement and colour-wheel attempts, mock-exam results, your country and trade specialisation and your exam date are stored on your device and in your private iCloud (Apple CloudKit), so they sync between your own devices; with iCloud switched off they stay on the device only. Nothing is uploaded to us. A mistakes PDF is created on the device and shared only when you choose to. Deleting the app deletes the data on that device; iCloud data can be removed in the iOS Settings app.

Permissions and device features

Notifications (optional): a local study reminder on the days and time you choose, scheduled on the device; no push server. Read-aloud uses the speech voices built into iOS on the device. The widget reads only from the app's own shared storage on the device. The app uses no camera, microphone, location, photos or health data.

Usage statistics (off by default)

If you switch on Settings › Usage statistics, we use Google Analytics for Firebase (without the advertising identifier), provided by Google Ireland Ltd. It then collects which screens and buttons are used, in-app purchase events, the approximate country/region Google derives from the IP address, and basic technical data (app and iOS version, device model). Events never contain your answers, calculations or exam date. The data is not linked to your identity and not used for tracking or advertising; Google processes it for us and keeps it for at most 14 months. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time by switching it off; Google may process data in the USA under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Purchases

The full version of Maler Prüfung is a one-time purchase handled by Apple via the App Store. We receive no payment details; the app only asks Apple whether the purchase is active. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Independence

Maler Prüfung is an independent learning tool and is not affiliated with any Handwerkskammer, Innung, Wirtschaftskammer, vocational school or examination authority. It does not issue any certificate. The exam procedure and grading are set by the responsible body in your country.

Your rights and contact

You can delete any item in the app at any time; deleting the app removes everything stored on the device. You can withdraw consent to the usage statistics at any time by switching them off. Questions: utqsall@gmail.com. Controller: Mustafa Utku Bayık (BaytechBV).
