Privacy Policy – Fristwerk

2026-11-24

Summary

Fristwerk has no account and no server of ours. Your data stays on your device. The only data that can reach us is anonymous usage statistics, which you can switch off.

Your data

Your files, deadlines, preliminary deadlines, check steps and notes are stored on your device. Nothing is uploaded to us. Deleting the app deletes the data on that device. There is no cloud sync.

Permissions

Notifications (optional): local reminders for the preliminary deadline and the deadline day, scheduled on the device and shown without party names on the lock screen. Calendar export: you can save a deadline as a calendar file (.ics) and open it in the calendar app yourself; Fristwerk has no access to your calendar. Fristwerk uses no camera, microphone, location, contacts or health data.

Usage statistics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase (without the advertising identifier), provided by Google Ireland Ltd. It collects which screens and buttons are used, in-app purchase events, the approximate country/region Google derives from the IP address, and basic technical data (app and iOS version, device model). Events never contain file numbers, party names, dates or deadline details. The data is not linked to your identity and not used for tracking or advertising; Google processes it for us and keeps it for at most 14 months. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); Google may process data in the USA under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. See Google's Privacy Policy. Switch it off in the app: Settings › Usage statistics.

Purchases

Fristwerk Plus is an auto-renewable subscription handled by Apple via the App Store. We receive no payment details; the app only asks Apple whether the purchase is active. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Independence

Fristwerk is an independent calculation aid and not legal advice. It is not affiliated with any court, bar association or software vendor; checking every deadline remains the responsibility of the user.

Your rights and contact

You can delete any item in the app at any time; deleting the app removes everything stored on the device. You can object to the usage statistics at any time by switching them off (Art. 21 GDPR). Questions: utqsall@gmail.com. Controller: Mustafa Utku Bayık (BaytechBV).
