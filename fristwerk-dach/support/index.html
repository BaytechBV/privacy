Support – Fristwerk

Questions or problems? Email utqsall@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

How are public holidays handled?

Fristwerk calculates the statutory holidays per German state, Austrian state and Swiss canton from its own tables (Easter-based dates computed). If a deadline ends on a weekend or public holiday, it moves to the next working day where the law says so.

Can my office see the same deadlines?

Yes, with Plus you can share your deadline book through iCloud with the people you invite.

How do I restore or cancel Plus?

Restore: Settings › Restore purchases, with the same Apple ID. Cancel: Settings app › your name › Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before renewal.

Where is my data?

On your device and in your private iCloud. There is no account and no server of ours.
