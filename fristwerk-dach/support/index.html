Support – Fristwerk

Questions or problems? Email utqsall@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

How are public holidays handled?

Fristwerk calculates the statutory holidays of all 16 German states, Austria and the Swiss cantons Zurich, Bern, Geneva, Jura and Ticino from its own tables (Easter-based dates computed on the device). If a deadline ends on a weekend or public holiday, it moves to the next working day where the law says so.

Can my office see the same deadlines?

Fristwerk has no account and no sync. With Plus you can export the deadline book as PDF, CSV or calendar file (ICS) and pass it on by email, AirDrop or into your office software.

How do I restore or cancel Plus?

Restore: Settings › Restore purchases, with the same Apple ID. Cancel: Settings app › your name › Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before renewal.

Where is my data?

Only on your device. There is no account, no cloud sync and no server of ours.

Is this legal advice?

No. Fristwerk is a calculation tool; checking every deadline remains the responsibility of the office.
