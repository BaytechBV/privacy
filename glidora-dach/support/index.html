Support – Glidora

Questions or problems? Email utqsall@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Which exam does Glidora cover?

The theory part of the paragliding licence (A-Schein / Brevet): air law, meteorology, flight theory and practical flying knowledge, written by us and checked against the published exam subjects.

Does Glidora replace my flight school?

No. It is for theory practice between lessons. Practical training and the exam itself happen with your school and the examining body.

How do I restore or cancel Plus?

Restore: Settings › Restore purchases, with the same Apple ID.

Where is my data?

On your device and in your private iCloud. There is no account and no server of ours.
