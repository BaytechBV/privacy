Privacy Policy – Glidora

2026-11-24

Summary

Glidora has no account and no server of ours. Your data stays on your device. The only data that can reach us is anonymous usage statistics, which you can switch off.

Your data

Your learning progress, answers, flagged questions and mock-exam results are stored only on your device. They are not synced and not sent to us. Nothing is uploaded to us. Deleting the app deletes all of this data.

Permissions

Notifications (optional): a local question of the day in the evening and a reminder before your theory exam, scheduled on the device; no push server. Glidora uses no camera, microphone, location, photos or health data. The age band you choose (14–17 / 18+) stays on the device and is not sent to analytics.

Usage statistics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase (without the advertising identifier), provided by Google Ireland Ltd. It collects which screens and buttons are used, in-app purchase events, the approximate country/region Google derives from the IP address, and basic technical data (app and iOS version, device model). Events never contain your answers or exam dates. The data is not linked to your identity and not used for tracking or advertising; Google processes it for us and keeps it for at most 14 months. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); Google may process data in the USA under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. See Google's Privacy Policy. Switch it off in the app: Settings › Usage statistics.

Purchases

Glidora Plus is a one-time purchase handled by Apple via the App Store. We receive no payment details; the app only asks Apple whether the purchase is active. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Independence

Glidora is an independent learning tool and is not affiliated with the DHV, SHV, ÖAeC, FFVL or any examining authority. The official question catalogue and your flight school always prevail.

Your rights and contact

You can delete any item in the app at any time; deleting the app removes everything stored on the device. You can object to the usage statistics at any time by switching them off (Art. 21 GDPR). Questions: utqsall@gmail.com. Controller: Mustafa Utku Bayık (BaytechBV).
