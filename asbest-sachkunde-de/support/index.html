Support – Asbest Sachkunde

Questions or problems? Email utqsall@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Which course does the app cover?

Practice for the test at the end of the asbestos Sachkunde course under TRGS 519: the large course (Anlage 3: demolition, remediation, maintenance) or Anlage 4C (small-scale ASI work). Questions are written by us, not original exam questions.

Do I get a certificate from the app?

No. The Sachkundenachweis is issued only by an accredited course with its exam. The app is for practice before and after the course days.

How do I restore or cancel Plus?

Restore: Settings › Restore purchases, with the same Apple ID.

Where is my data?

On your device and in your private iCloud. There is no account and no server of ours.
