Privacy Policy – Plainhunt

Last updated: 2026-11-22

Summary

Plainhunt has no account and no server of ours. Everything you do in the app stays on your device. The only data that reaches us is anonymous usage statistics, which you can switch off.

Your data

Methods, blue lines, place bells and touches are computed on your device from place notation. Your own methods, review cards and schedule, ringing-practice and striking sessions, tap-timing calibration and your peal book (towers, methods, dates, footnotes) are stored only on your device. There is no iCloud sync and no sharing to a server; deleting the app deletes this data. If you export or share an entry yourself with the system share sheet, it goes only where you send it.

Permissions

Plainhunt asks for no microphone, camera, location, contacts or photo access. Bell sounds are synthesised on the device; nothing is recorded. Notifications are optional: only if you switch on the practice-night reminder does the app ask for permission and schedule a local reminder on your device; no push server is involved.

Usage statistics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase (FirebaseAnalyticsCore, without the advertising identifier), provided by Google Ireland Ltd. It collects: a random app instance ID and the vendor identifier of the app (not the advertising ID, no App Tracking Transparency prompt), which screens and buttons are used and steps in the app (for example method opened, blue line viewed, review card answered, practice started or finished with the number of blows and an accuracy range, peal logged), in-app purchase events (product and price), the approximate location (country/region) that Google derives from the IP address, and basic technical and diagnostic data (app and iOS version, device model, Firebase Installations). Events never contain tower names, ringer names, footnotes or other text you enter. The data is not linked to your identity and not used for tracking or advertising; Google processes it for us and keeps it for at most 14 months. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); Google may process data in the USA under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. See Google's Privacy Policy. Switch it off in the app: Settings › Share anonymous usage.

Purchases

Plainhunt Plus is an auto-renewable subscription handled by Apple via the App Store. We receive no payment details; the app only asks Apple whether a subscription is active. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Independence

Plainhunt is an independent app and is not affiliated with any ringing society or guild.

Your rights and contact

Deleting an entry in the app removes it; deleting the app removes everything stored on your device. You can object to the usage statistics at any time by switching them off (Art. 21 GDPR). Questions: utqsall@gmail.com. Controller: Mustafa Utku Bayık (BaytechBV).
