Frequently asked questions

How are the blue lines worked out?

Every method is stored as place notation. Plainhunt applies each change row by row on your device, so the blue line for your bell, the place bells, the lead heads and the work at every lead end are computed, not drawn by hand. Calls (bobs and singles) change the lead-end change, and touches are checked for truth (no row repeated before rounds).

How do I write my own method?

Open Place notation › Write your own method, choose the stage and type the notation, for example x16x16x16,12 (x or - = all pairs swap, digits = places made, 0 = 10, E = 11, T = 12, a comma means each part is read forwards and back). Plainhunt checks every change and shows the lead, lead head and plain course before you save it.

What is tap-timing calibration?

Every device has some delay between a sound being played and reaching your ears, and between your touch and the app receiving it. In Settings › Calibrate tap timing you tap along with clicks and Plainhunt subtracts the measured offset from your striking. The milliseconds shown are an estimate: their accuracy is limited by your device's touch and audio latency.

My striking looks late with Bluetooth headphones

Bluetooth audio adds a long and changing delay. For practice use the speaker or wired headphones, and calibrate again after changing the output.

How do I restore my subscription?

Open the Plus page or Settings and tap “Restore purchases”. Use the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

How do I cancel Plainhunt Plus?

Open the Settings app › your name › Subscriptions › Plainhunt, at least 24 hours before the renewal date. Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

I found a wrong method

Please email utqsall@gmail.com with the method name and stage (Settings › Method data and sources › Report a wrong method opens an email for you). Method names and place notation are entered by hand and checked; every report is checked again.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. There is no account, no iCloud sync and no server of ours.

How do I switch off usage statistics?

Settings › Share anonymous usage.
