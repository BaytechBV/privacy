Learn the method for your own bell.

Plainhunt is a method book and practice tower in your pocket for change ringers. Choose your stage and your bell, and Plainhunt draws the blue line of the method you are learning from its place notation, works out your place bells and the work at every lead end, and helps you remember it with a few review cards a night. Then ring it: tap your bell in the bell circle while the other bells ring evenly, and see how early or late each blow was. Log your quarters and peals in the peal book. Works offline, no account.
