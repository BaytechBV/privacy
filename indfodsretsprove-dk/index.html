Privacy Policy – Indfødsretsprøven

Last updated: 2026-10-15

Who we are

This app is published by Mustafa Utku Bayık. Contact: utqsall@gmail.com.

What data the app processes

The app does not require an account. Your progress, settings and mock test results are stored only on your device. The app sends no data to us or anyone else: no usage statistics, no ads, no tracking. It works offline.

Purchases

The one-time in-app purchase is handled entirely by Apple via the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Ratings

The app may ask you to rate it using Apple's standard rating dialog. Ratings are handled by Apple.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all data it stored on your device. You can also reset your progress in Settings.

Your rights

If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us at the address above. We do not hold any data about you.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
