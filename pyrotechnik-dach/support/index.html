Support – Pyrotechnik Prüfung

Questions or problems? Email utqsall@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Which courses does the app cover?

Theory practice for the basic course for display fireworks (F4) and stage pyrotechnics (T2) in Germany and Austria. Questions are written by us, not original exam questions.

Are the distance tables binding?

No. They are guide values for learning. The manufacturer's instructions, the permit conditions and the authority always prevail.

How do I restore or cancel Plus?

Restore: Settings › Restore purchases, with the same Apple ID.

Where is my data?

On your device and in your private iCloud. There is no account and no server of ours.
