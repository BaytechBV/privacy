Support – Etiqua

Questions or problems? Email utqsall@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Where do the nutrient values come from?

From the open French CIQUAL table and the USDA FoodData Central SR Legacy table, built into the app. You can also enter values from your supplier's data sheet.

Are the 14 allergens covered?

Yes. Etiqua marks the 14 allergens of the EU food information regulation in the ingredient list.

How do I restore or cancel Plus?

Restore: Settings › Restore purchases, with the same Apple ID. Cancel: Settings app › your name › Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before renewal.

Where is my data?

On your device and in your private iCloud. There is no account and no server of ours.
