Support – Ordinationsassistenz Prüfung

Questions or problems? Email utqsall@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Which exam does the app cover?

Practice for the final commission exam of the Ordinationsassistenz training in Austria (MAB-Gesetz): hygiene and reprocessing, anatomy, first aid, laboratory, e-card and billing, law and communication. All questions and cases are written by us, not original exam questions.

Do I get a certificate from the app?

No. The qualification is awarded only by your course provider after the official exam. The app is for practice during the course.

How do I restore the full version?

Settings › Restore purchases, with the same Apple ID.

Where is my data?

On your device and in your private iCloud. There is no account and no server of ours.
