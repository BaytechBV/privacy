Support – Moltura

Questions or problems? Email utqsall@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Why does fresh-milled flour need more water?

Whole-grain flour with all the bran absorbs more water and needs time to soak. Moltura shows the change and why, per grain and grind.

Which mills are supported?

Any home mill. Choose fine, medium or coarse; Moltura names no mill brands.

How do I restore or cancel Plus?

Restore: Settings › Restore purchases, with the same Apple ID. Cancel: Settings app › your name › Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before renewal.

Where is my data?

Only on your device; you can export the baking book as CSV in Settings › Your data and export. There is no account and no server of ours.
