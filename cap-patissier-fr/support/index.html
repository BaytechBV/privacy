Support – CAP Pâtissier – Révisions

Questions or problems? Email utqsall@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Which exam does the app cover?

Revision for the written parts of the CAP Pâtissier (EP1 and EP2) in France, for candidats libres, adults retraining and apprentices; also usable in Belgium, Switzerland and Luxembourg. All questions, technical sheets and base recipes are written by us, not original exam papers.

Can a parent or my training centre buy it for me?

The full version is a one-time purchase with Family Sharing; a parent can buy it on their Apple ID and share it via Family Sharing.

How do I restore the full version?

Settings › Restore purchases, with the same Apple ID.

Where is my data?

On your device (and in your private iCloud if sync is on). There is no account and no server of ours.
