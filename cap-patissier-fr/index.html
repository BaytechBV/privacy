Privacy Policy – CAP Pâtissier – Révisions

2026-11-27

Summary

CAP Pâtissier – Révisions has no account and no server of ours. Your data stays on your device and, if iCloud is switched on, in your private iCloud. Anonymous usage statistics are switched off by default; only if you switch them on can anonymous usage data reach us.

Data on your device

Your learning progress, answers, open mistakes, production-planning (ordonnancement) attempts, mock-exam results and your exam date are stored on your device; if the app offers iCloud sync and iCloud is on, they sync through your private iCloud (Apple CloudKit) between your own devices only. Nothing is uploaded to us. A mistakes PDF is created on the device and shared only when you choose to. Deleting the app deletes the data on that device; iCloud data can be removed in the iOS Settings app.

Permissions and device features

Notifications (optional): a local study reminder at the time you choose, scheduled on the device; no push server. Read-aloud uses the speech voices built into iOS on the device. The widget reads only from the app's own shared storage on the device. The app uses no camera, microphone, location, photos or health data.

Usage statistics (off by default)

If you switch on Settings › Usage statistics, we use Google Analytics for Firebase (without the advertising identifier), provided by Google Ireland Ltd. It then collects which screens and buttons are used, in-app purchase events, the approximate country/region Google derives from the IP address, and basic technical data (app and iOS version, device model). Events never contain your answers or exam date. The data is not linked to your identity and not used for tracking or advertising; Google processes it for us and keeps it for at most 14 months. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time by switching it off; Google may process data in the USA under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Purchases

The full version of CAP Pâtissier – Révisions is a one-time purchase handled by Apple via the App Store. We receive no payment details; the app only asks Apple whether the purchase is active. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Independence

CAP Pâtissier – Révisions is an independent learning tool and is not affiliated with the Éducation nationale, any CFA, chamber of trades or examination authority. It does not issue any certificate. The exam procedure and grading are set by the official texts and the jury of your académie.

Your rights and contact

You can delete any item in the app at any time; deleting the app removes everything stored on the device. You can withdraw consent to the usage statistics at any time by switching them off. Questions: utqsall@gmail.com. Controller: Mustafa Utku Bayık (BaytechBV).
