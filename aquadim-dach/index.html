Privacy Policy – Aquadim

2026-11-24

Summary

Aquadim has no account and no server of ours. Your data stays on your device. The only data that can reach us is anonymous usage statistics, which you can switch off.

Your data

Your projects, buildings, pipe sections, fixtures, calculation results and flushing reports (PDF) are stored only on your device. There is no cloud sync. If you export a PDF or CSV file and share it, you decide who receives it. Nothing is uploaded to us. Deleting the app deletes the data.

Permissions

Aquadim asks for no permissions: no camera, microphone, location, contacts or health data.

Usage statistics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase (without the advertising identifier), provided by Google Ireland Ltd. It collects which screens and buttons are used, in-app purchase events, the approximate country/region Google derives from the IP address, and basic technical data (app and iOS version, device model). Events never contain project names, addresses or calculated values. The data is not linked to your identity and not used for tracking or advertising; Google processes it for us and keeps it for at most 14 months. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); Google may process data in the USA under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. See Google's Privacy Policy. Switch it off in the app: Settings › Usage statistics.

Purchases

Aquadim Plus is an auto-renewable subscription handled by Apple via the App Store. We receive no payment details; the app only asks Apple whether the purchase is active. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Independence

Aquadim is an independent calculation aid. It describes the calculation method in its own words and does not replace the standard (DIN 1988-300, ÖNORM B 2531, SVGW W3) or professional design.

Your rights and contact

You can delete any item in the app at any time; deleting the app removes everything stored on the device. You can object to the usage statistics at any time by switching them off (Art. 21 GDPR). Questions: utqsall@gmail.com. Controller: Mustafa Utku Bayık (BaytechBV).
