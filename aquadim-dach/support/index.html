Support – Aquadim

Questions or problems? Email utqsall@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Which method does Aquadim use?

The peak-flow method with loading units, simultaneity, flow velocity and pressure loss per section, as used for drinking-water installations in Germany, Austria and Switzerland. The way the result is reached is shown step by step.

Can I pass a calculation to a colleague?

Yes, with Plus you can export the report as PDF or the section table as CSV and send it via Mail, Messages or Files. There is no cloud sync.

How do I restore or cancel Plus?

Restore: Settings › Restore purchases, with the same Apple ID. Cancel: Settings app › your name › Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before renewal.

Where is my data?

Only on your device. There is no account and no server of ours.
