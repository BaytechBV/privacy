Privacy Policy – Golf Green Card

Last updated: 2026-10-28

Who we are

This app is published by Mustafa Utku Bayık. Contact: utqsall@gmail.com.

What data the app processes

The app does not require an account. Your progress, settings, checklist ticks and mock exam results are stored only on your device. It works offline.

Usage statistics

To improve the app we use Google Analytics for Firebase (Google Ireland Ltd.). It receives which screens are opened, which buttons are tapped and events such as a finished mock exam (score only as a range) or a purchase, together with a random app-instance ID, device and app version, an approximate location derived from the IP address, and diagnostic data. No name, no account, no answers or other text you enter, no advertising ID; the data is not linked to you and not used for tracking or ads. Google keeps event data for 2 months. You can turn this off at any time in Settings → "Send usage statistics". Legal basis: legitimate interest in improving the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). See Google's Privacy Policy.

Purchases

The one-time in-app purchase is handled entirely by Apple via the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Ratings

The app may ask you to rate it using Apple's standard rating dialog. Ratings are handled by Apple.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all data it stored on your device. You can also reset your progress in Settings.

Your rights

If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us at the address above. We hold no data that identifies you; the usage statistics above are anonymous.

Changes

We may change this policy. The date above shows the current version.
