Privacy Policy – Hlasko

Last updated: 2026-11-03

Summary

Hlasko has no account and no server of ours. What you enter stays on your iPhone. The only data that reaches us is anonymous usage statistics, which you can switch off.

Your data

The microphone is used only when you start Hlasko. Speech is recognised on your iPhone; the sound is not recorded and not sent to us or to speech-recognition servers. Reminders and events go into your own Reminders and Calendar apps, contacts are read only on the phone, and a message is never sent on its own – you always send it yourself.

Shopping list

The shopping list is a list in your Reminders app. If you share it with your family in Reminders, Apple syncs it through your iCloud – Hlasko has no server for it and no access. Your own commands, the Today history and remembered names stay on this phone only.

Usage statistics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase (FirebaseAnalyticsCore, without the advertising identifier), provided by Google Ireland Ltd. It collects: a random app instance ID created by the app (not the advertising ID), which screens and buttons are used and the steps of the funnel (for example command recognised, reminder saved, message prepared), in-app purchase events (product and price), the approximate location (country/region) that Google derives from the IP address, and technical installation data (Firebase Installations). Events never contain your words, contact names, list items or other text you enter. The data is not linked to you and not used for tracking or advertising; Google processes it for us and keeps it for at most 14 months. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); Google may process data in the USA under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Switch it off in the app: Settings › Send usage statistics.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. We receive no payment details; the app only asks Apple whether the purchase is unlocked.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone; nothing is sent to us. This also applies to the timer.

Your rights and contact

Deleting an entry in the app removes it; deleting the app removes everything stored on your iPhone. You can object to the usage statistics at any time by switching them off (Art. 21 GDPR). Questions: utqsall@gmail.com. Controller: Mustafa Utku Bayık (BaytechBV).
