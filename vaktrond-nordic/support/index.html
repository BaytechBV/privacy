Support – Vaktrond

Questions or problems? Email utqsall@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Which courses does Vaktrond cover?

Theory practice for security guard and public order guard training in Sweden, Norway, Finland and Denmark, with questions, cases and law cards written by us and checked against the published course subjects.

Does Vaktrond replace my course?

No. It is for practice between lessons. The course, the practical parts and the final test happen with your training provider.

How do I restore or cancel Plus?

Restore: Settings › Restore purchases, with the same Apple ID.

Where is my data?

On your device and in your private iCloud. There is no account and no server of ours.
