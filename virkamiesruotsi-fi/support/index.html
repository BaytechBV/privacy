Support – Virkamiesruotsi

Questions or problems? Email utqsall@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Which exam does the app cover?

Practice for the Swedish language test for civil servants and students in Finland (virkamiesruotsi, written and oral part): grammar, vocabulary, reading, writing and conversation, with content written by us.

Is my speech recorded?

No. Speech is recognised on your iPhone only to show which words you said correctly. Nothing is stored or sent.

How do I restore or cancel Plus?

Restore: Settings › Restore purchases, with the same Apple ID.

Where is my data?

On your device and in your private iCloud. There is no account and no server of ours.
