Privacy Policy – Virkamiesruotsi

2026-11-24

Summary

Virkamiesruotsi has no account and no server of ours. Your data stays on your device and in your private iCloud. The only data that can reach us is anonymous usage statistics, which you can switch off.

Your data

Your learning progress, answers, open mistakes, mock-exam results and your own writing drafts are stored on your device and in your private iCloud (Apple CloudKit), so they sync between your own devices. Nothing is uploaded to us. Deleting the app deletes the data on that device; iCloud data can be removed in the iOS Settings app.

Permissions

Microphone and speech recognition (optional): only in the conversation cards while you practise speaking Swedish; your speech is recognised on the device, nothing is recorded or sent to a server. Notifications (optional): a local card of the day and a reminder before your exam date, scheduled on the device; no push server. The app uses no camera, location, photos or health data.

Usage statistics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase (without the advertising identifier), provided by Google Ireland Ltd. It collects which screens and buttons are used, in-app purchase events, the approximate country/region Google derives from the IP address, and basic technical data (app and iOS version, device model). Events never contain your answers, speech, writing drafts or exam date. The data is not linked to your identity and not used for tracking or advertising; Google processes it for us and keeps it for at most 14 months. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); Google may process data in the USA under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. See Google's Privacy Policy. Switch it off in the app: Settings › Usage statistics.

Purchases

Virkamiesruotsi Plus is a one-time purchase handled by Apple via the App Store. We receive no payment details; the app only asks Apple whether the purchase is active. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Independence

Virkamiesruotsi is an independent learning tool and is not affiliated with any university, language centre, the Finnish National Agency for Education or any examining body. Your course material and the examiner always prevail.

Your rights and contact

You can delete any item in the app at any time; deleting the app removes everything stored on the device. You can object to the usage statistics at any time by switching them off (Art. 21 GDPR). Questions: utqsall@gmail.com. Controller: Mustafa Utku Bayık (BaytechBV).
