Support – Palote

Questions or problems? Email utqsall@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Is my child's data safe?

Nicknames, sessions and progress stay on your devices and in your private iCloud. Purchases and settings are behind an adult gate. There are no ads and no accounts.

The school worksheet does it differently.

Exercises are generated by the app following the usual ABN steps. If your child's worksheet uses a different layout, follow the worksheet.

How do I restore or cancel Plus?

Restore: Settings › Restore purchases, with the same Apple ID. Cancel: Settings app › your name › Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before renewal.

Where is my data?

On your device and in your private iCloud. There is no account and no server of ours.
