Privacy Policy – Palote

2026-11-23

Summary

Palote has no account and no server of ours. Your data stays on your device and in your private iCloud. The only data that can reach us is anonymous usage statistics, which you can switch off.

Your data

Your children's nicknames, school year, daily sessions, answers and progress are stored on your device and in your private iCloud (Apple CloudKit), so they sync between your own devices. If you share an item through iCloud, only the people you invite can see it. Nothing is uploaded to us. Deleting the app deletes the data on that device; iCloud data can be removed in the iOS Settings app.

Permissions

Notifications (optional): one local reminder a day at the time you choose, scheduled on the device, without the child's name on the lock screen. Palote uses no camera, microphone, location, photos or health data. Handwriting on the grid is recognised on the device and never leaves it.

Usage statistics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase (without the advertising identifier), provided by Google Ireland Ltd. It collects which screens and buttons are used, in-app purchase events, the approximate country/region Google derives from the IP address, and basic technical data (app and iOS version, device model). Analytics is off by default; a parent can switch it on in Settings (behind the adult gate). Events are parent-level only and never contain a child's name, age, school year, answers or handwriting. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. The data is not linked to your identity and not used for tracking or advertising; Google processes it for us and keeps it for at most 14 months. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); Google may process data in the USA under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. See Google's Privacy Policy. Switch it on or off in the app's Settings.

Purchases

Palote Plus is an auto-renewable subscription handled by Apple via the App Store. We receive no payment details; the app only asks Apple whether the purchase is active. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Independence

Palote is an independent practice tool for the ABN method and is not affiliated with any school, publisher or education authority.

Your rights and contact

You can delete any item in the app at any time; deleting the app removes everything stored on the device. You can object to the usage statistics at any time by switching them off (Art. 21 GDPR). Questions: utqsall@gmail.com. Controller: Mustafa Utku Bayık (BaytechBV).
