Privacy Policy – Curalo

2026-11-24

Summary

Curalo has no account and no server of ours. Your data stays on your device. The only data that can reach us is anonymous usage statistics, which you can switch off.

Your data

Your sites, pours, temperature readings, checklists, your own strength curves and cure reports (PDF) are stored only on your device (there is no iCloud sync). You can export the temperature log (CSV) or a protocol (PDF) yourself through the iOS share sheet. Nothing is uploaded to us. Deleting the app (or Settings › Data on this iPhone › Delete all data) removes it; iOS may include it in your own device backup.

Permissions

Location (optional, only while in use): to load the 72-hour forecast for your site from Apple WeatherKit; the coordinates go to Apple for the forecast and are not stored or sent to us. Weather data: Apple Weather. Notifications (optional): local frost and stripping reminders; no push server. Curalo uses no camera, microphone or health data.

Usage statistics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase (without the advertising identifier), provided by Google Ireland Ltd. It collects which screens and buttons are used, in-app purchase events, the approximate country/region Google derives from the IP address, and basic technical data (app and iOS version, device model). Events never contain coordinates, site names or measured values. The data is not linked to your identity and not used for tracking or advertising; Google processes it for us and keeps it for at most 14 months. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); Google may process data in the USA under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. See Google's Privacy Policy. Switch it off in the app: Settings › Usage analysis.

Purchases

Curalo Plus is an auto-renewable subscription handled by Apple via the App Store. We receive no payment details; the app only asks Apple whether the purchase is active. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Independence

Curalo is an independent tool. Strength values are estimates by the maturity method and replace neither testing nor design; site management decides on stripping and frost protection.

Your rights and contact

You can delete any item in the app at any time; deleting the app removes everything stored on the device. You can object to the usage statistics at any time by switching them off (Art. 21 GDPR). Questions: utqsall@gmail.com. Controller: Mustafa Utku Bayık (BaytechBV).
