Support – Curalo

Questions or problems? Email utqsall@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

How is strength estimated?

Maturity by Nurse-Saul (temperature × time above a datum temperature) and a typical strength curve for the cement class; your own cube results can calibrate the curve.

Can I share a site with my team?

Curalo keeps everything on your iPhone (no account, no cloud sync). Share the temperature log as CSV (Settings › Data on this iPhone) or the curing protocol as PDF (Plus) through the iOS share sheet.

How do I restore or cancel Plus?

Restore: Settings › Restore purchases, with the same Apple ID. Cancel: Settings app › your name › Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before renewal.

Where is my data?

Only on your device. There is no account and no server of ours.
