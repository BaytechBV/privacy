How is the course length measured?

Along the dog's line you draw with your finger: Hurdla smooths the line and adds up its length in metres on the scaled ring. Distances between obstacles are measured from where the dog leaves one obstacle to where it reaches the next (hurdles: the bar; tunnel, contacts, weave poles: their ends).

How are SCT and MCT calculated?

SCT = length ÷ the speed you choose (the method for national trials), rounded to the nearest second. MCT = length ÷ 2.5 m/s in Agility and ÷ 3.0 m/s in Jumping. At FCI international trials the judge sets the SCT from the fastest dog with the fewest faults + 15 %.

Which rules does the rule check use?

Our own summary of the FCI Agility Regulations (valid from 1 July 2025): ring at least 20 × 40 m, 100–220 m, 15–22 obstacles, at least 7 jumps, at most 5 tunnel performances, weave poles once, three contact types in Agility, 5–9 m on the dog's line, at most 7 m in a straight line, 1 m between obstacles, 6 m before the start and after the finish. National federations can differ. Hurdla checks the plan; the judge decides.

How do I share a course with my group?

Share › Send as PDF for everyone, or Send course file (.hurdla, Plus): whoever opens the file in Hurdla gets the same ring, numbers and dog's line. Everything goes through the iOS share sheet; there is no account and no server.

Where is my data? Is there a backup?

On your device only. Device backups (iCloud or computer backups of your iPhone) include the app's data. Course files are a handy copy of single courses.

What is free, what is Plus?

Without Plus: 2 saved courses, all obstacles, the dog's line with length and SCT, the rule check and the PDF with a watermark. Plus: unlimited courses, templates, training plan, run log and progress per dog, course files and the PDF without watermark.

How do I restore or cancel Plus?

Restore: paywall or Settings › Restore purchases, with the same Apple ID. Cancel: Settings app › your name › Subscriptions › Hurdla, at least 24 hours before renewal.
