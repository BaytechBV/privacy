Privacy Policy – Hurdla

2026-11-22

Summary

Hurdla has no account and no server of ours. Your courses, dogs, training plan and runs stay on your device. The only data that reaches us is anonymous usage statistics, which you can switch off.

Your data

Courses (ring size, obstacles, numbers, the dog's line, notes), dogs (name, breed, height at the withers), training sessions and timed runs are stored only on your device. There is no iCloud sync and no upload. Deleting the app deletes this data. PDF course sheets and course files (.hurdla) are created on the device and go only where you send them with the share sheet; whoever receives a course file gets the course you sent, nothing else.

Permissions

Notifications (optional): local reminders one hour before a training session, scheduled on your device; no push server. Hurdla asks for no other permission (no camera, location, contacts, photos or health data).

Usage statistics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase (FirebaseAnalyticsCore, without the advertising identifier), provided by Google Ireland Ltd. It collects: a random app instance ID (not the advertising ID, no App Tracking Transparency prompt), which screens and buttons are used and steps in the app (for example obstacle added with its type, dog's line drawn with a length range, rule check opened with the number of hints, PDF shared), in-app purchase events (plan), the approximate location (country/region) Google derives from the IP address, and basic technical and diagnostic data (app and iOS version, device model). Events never contain course or dog names, people, notes or run times. The data is not linked to your identity and not used for tracking or advertising; Google processes it for us and keeps it for at most 14 months. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); Google may process data in the USA under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. See Google's Privacy Policy. Switch it off in the app: Settings › Usage statistics.

Purchases

Hurdla Plus is an auto-renewable subscription handled by Apple via the App Store. We receive no payment details; the app only asks Apple whether a subscription is active. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Independence

Hurdla is an independent planning tool. Obstacle sizes and course rules are our own summary of the FCI Agility Regulations (valid from 1 July 2025). Hurdla is not affiliated with the FCI or any federation and does not replace the judge.

Your rights and contact

Deleting a course, dog or run in the app removes it; deleting the app removes everything stored on your device. You can object to the usage statistics at any time by switching them off (Art. 21 GDPR). Questions: utqsall@gmail.com. Controller: Mustafa Utku Bayık (BaytechBV).
