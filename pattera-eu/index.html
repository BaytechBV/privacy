Privacy – Pattera

Updated: 2026-11-19

The short version

Pattera has no account and no server of ours. Your drawings, journal tiles, photos, notes, collections and practice days are stored only on your iPhone. The only thing we receive is anonymous usage statistics, and you can switch those off.

Drawings, photos and the camera

Tiles you draw on screen and paper tiles you scan or pick from Photos are saved in the app's own storage on your iPhone (and in your device backup if you use one). There is no sync and nothing is uploaded. The camera is used only when you tap “Scan tile” (Apple's document scanner); choosing a photo uses the iOS photo picker, which gives Pattera only the photo you pick. A tile leaves your iPhone only if you share or print it yourself.

Reminders

Daily reminders and pack reminders are local notifications created on your iPhone. There is no push server.

Usage statistics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase (FirebaseAnalyticsCore, without the advertising identifier) by Google Ireland Ltd. It records a random app-instance ID, which screens and buttons are used, steps in the app (for example pattern opened, step done, tile saved, paywall shown), purchase events, the approximate country or region (derived by Google from the IP address) and technical data such as app version, iOS version and device model. It never contains drawings, photos, notes or anything you type. The data is not linked to you and is not used for tracking or advertising. It is kept for up to 14 months. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); Google may process data in the USA under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Switch off any time: Settings › Anonymous usage statistics.

Purchases

Pattera Plus is a one-time purchase handled by Apple. We never see payment details; the app only asks Apple whether this Apple Account owns Pattera Plus.

Your rights and contact

Deleting a tile removes it from the iPhone; deleting the app removes all its data. You can object to the statistics by switching them off (Art. 21 GDPR) and ask us about your rights at utqsall@gmail.com. Controller: Mustafa Utku Bayık (BaytechBV).

Eine Kachel verlässt das iPhone nur, wenn du sie selbst teilst oder druckst.

Erinnerungen

Tägliche Erinnerungen und Paket-Erinnerungen sind lokale Mitteilungen, die dein iPhone selbst plant. Es gibt keinen Push-Server.

Erfasst werden eine zufällige Instanz-ID, genutzte Bildschirme und Tasten, Schritte in der App (z. B. Muster geöffnet, Schritt erledigt, Kachel gespeichert, Kaufseite gezeigt), Kaufereignisse, das ungefähre Land bzw. die Region (von Google aus der IP-Adresse abgeleitet) sowie technische Daten wie App-, iOS-Version und Gerätemodell. Niemals Zeichnungen, Fotos, Notizen oder Eingaben. Die Daten sind nicht mit dir verknüpft und werden nicht für Tracking oder Werbung genutzt; Speicherdauer höchstens 14 Monate. Rechtsgrundlage: berechtigtes Interesse an der Verbesserung der App (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. f DSGVO); Google kann Daten in den USA nach dem EU-US Data Privacy Framework verarbeiten. Käufe
