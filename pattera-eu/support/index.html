Help – Pattera

Questions or something not working? Write to utqsall@gmail.com.

How do I restore Pattera Plus?

Settings › Restore purchases, or the paywall › Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Is Pattera Plus a subscription?

No. It is a one-time purchase. Nothing renews.

What works without Plus?

20 patterns, the pattern of the day (with the guided canvas), 10 journal tiles, the streak and the daily reminder.

The scanner does not find the edges.

Lay the tile on a dark table, use a lamp or window and keep your shadow off the paper. You can always choose a photo instead.

Where are my tiles stored?

Only on this iPhone. Use Share or print on a tile to keep a copy elsewhere.

When do the packs open?

Frost & Lantern on 1 December 2026, Ink Garden on 1 January 2027. They are already inside the app and open on that day on your device.
