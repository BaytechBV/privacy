Support – Vorlauf

Questions or problems? Email utqsall@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Is home distilling allowed?

That depends on your country. Vorlauf is meant for licensed or lump-sum distillers who may distil legally; please follow the rules of your customs authority.

How accurate is the dilution?

Vorlauf corrects for temperature and contraction using published alcohol tables. Always check the final strength with a calibrated hydrometer.

How do I restore or cancel Plus?

Restore: Settings › Restore purchases, with the same Apple ID. Cancel: Settings app › your name › Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before renewal.

Where is my data?

Only on your device. There is no account, no cloud sync and no server of ours. Export the season book as PDF or CSV to keep a copy.
