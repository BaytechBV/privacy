Privacy Policy – Vorlauf

2026-11-24

Summary

Vorlauf has no account and no server of ours. Your data stays on your device and in your private iCloud. The only data that can reach us is anonymous usage statistics, which you can switch off.

Your data

Your mashes, fermentation notes, distillation runs, cuts, dilutions and batch labels are stored on your device and in your private iCloud (Apple CloudKit), so they sync between your own devices. Nothing is uploaded to us. Deleting the app deletes the data on that device; iCloud data can be removed in the iOS Settings app.

Permissions

Notifications (optional): local reminders to check a fermentation, scheduled on the device; no push server. Vorlauf uses no camera, microphone, location, contacts or health data.

Usage statistics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase (without the advertising identifier), provided by Google Ireland Ltd. It collects which screens and buttons are used, in-app purchase events, the approximate country/region Google derives from the IP address, and basic technical data (app and iOS version, device model). Events never contain batch names, volumes or alcohol values. The data is not linked to your identity and not used for tracking or advertising; Google processes it for us and keeps it for at most 14 months. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); Google may process data in the USA under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. See Google's Privacy Policy. Switch it off in the app: Settings › Usage statistics.

Purchases

Vorlauf Plus is an auto-renewable subscription handled by Apple via the App Store. We receive no payment details; the app only asks Apple whether the purchase is active. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Independence

Vorlauf is an independent record book for licensed and lump-sum distillers (e.g. Abfindungsbrenner). It does not encourage unlicensed distilling. Tax figures are estimates; the customs office's assessment always prevails.

Your rights and contact

You can delete any item in the app at any time; deleting the app removes everything stored on the device. You can object to the usage statistics at any time by switching them off (Art. 21 GDPR). Questions: utqsall@gmail.com. Controller: Mustafa Utku Bayık (BaytechBV).
