Summary

Your data

The motion sensors are used only during a ski day that you start; your turns are calculated on the iPhone. Location is used only approximately and only during the ski day, to tell lift from run; it is not stored, and there is no map and no route. Runs and values stay on your device.

The data is not linked to you and not used for tracking or advertising; Google processes it for us and keeps it for at most 14 months. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); Google may process data in the USA under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. We receive no payment details; the app only asks Apple whether the purchase is unlocked.

Your rights and contact
