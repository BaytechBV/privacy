Support – Baricentro

Questions or problems? Email utqsall@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

How is the centre of gravity calculated?

From the wing geometry you enter (root and tip chord, sweep, span) Baricentro computes the mean aerodynamic chord and a CG range, typically 25–33 % of it.

Do I need NFC tags?

No. NFC is optional; you can also use printed QR labels or log flights by hand.

How do I restore or cancel Plus?

Restore: Settings › Restore purchases, with the same Apple ID. Cancel: Settings app › your name › Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before renewal.

Where is my data?

On your device. There is no account and no server of ours.
