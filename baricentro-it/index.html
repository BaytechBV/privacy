Privacy Policy – Baricentro

2026-11-24

Summary

Baricentro has no account and no server of ours. Your data stays on your device. The only data that can reach us is anonymous usage statistics, which you can switch off.

Your data

Your models, wing measurements, setup cards, battery packs and flight log are stored on your device. Nothing is uploaded to us. Deleting the app deletes the data on that device. There is no cloud sync.

Permissions

Camera (optional): only while you scan the QR label of a model or battery; no photo is stored. NFC (optional): to read and write the NFC tags on your models and batteries to log a flight with one tap; the tag holds only an in-app ID. Notifications (optional): local storage and maintenance reminders; no push server. Baricentro uses no location, microphone or health data.

Usage statistics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase (without the advertising identifier), provided by Google Ireland Ltd. It collects which screens and buttons are used, in-app purchase events, the approximate country/region Google derives from the IP address, and basic technical data (app and iOS version, device model). Events never contain model names, field names or measured values. The data is not linked to your identity and not used for tracking or advertising; Google processes it for us and keeps it for at most 14 months. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); Google may process data in the USA under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. See Google's Privacy Policy. Switch it off in the app: Settings › Usage statistics.

Purchases

Baricentro Plus is an auto-renewable subscription handled by Apple via the App Store. We receive no payment details; the app only asks Apple whether the purchase is active. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Independence

Baricentro is an independent tool. Centre-of-gravity values are calculated estimates from the wing geometry; the model manufacturer's instructions always prevail.

Your rights and contact

You can delete any item in the app at any time; deleting the app removes everything stored on the device. You can object to the usage statistics at any time by switching them off (Art. 21 GDPR). Questions: utqsall@gmail.com. Controller: Mustafa Utku Bayık (BaytechBV).
